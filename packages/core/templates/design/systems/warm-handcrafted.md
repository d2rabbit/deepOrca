# Design System: Warm Handcrafted

> Artisanal warmth — cream and terracotta, humanist serif display, stitched
> dashed borders, kraft-paper textures. The page feels made by hands.
> **When to pick this**: artisanal/DTC brands, food & craft, nonprofits, portfolios
> selling a human story. Avoid for hard-tech credibility or hacker aesthetics.

## Color

- Background: `#faf3e8` (cream)
- Surface: `#f1e6d4` (parchment)
- Accent: `#a34722` (terracotta — max 2 uses per screen)
- Text: `#3b2f25` (espresso)
- Muted: `#6f5f4e`
- Stitch border: 1px dashed `#d8c5a8`

## Typography

- Display: humanist serif (Iowan Old Style / Georgia), weight 700
- Body: humanist sans (Avenir / Segoe UI / system-ui), weight 400, line-height 1.7
- Labels/eyebrows: small caps sans, wide tracking — reads like a stamp

## Layout

- Max-width: 960px
- Section padding: 72px vertical
- Grid: 12-column, 24px gap
- Card radius: 12px (soft, not pill)
- Button radius: 8px

## Motion

- Transitions: 200-250ms ease — gentle, nothing snaps
- Hover: warm deepen (surface darkens one step) or slight scale(1.01)
- No lift shadows — elements settle rather than float

## Components

- Button primary: accent background, white text, 8px radius, hover deepens
- Button ghost: transparent, 1px dashed `#a34722` border, accent text
- Card: parchment surface, 1px dashed stitch border, 12px radius, 32px padding
- Image placeholder: kraft tone `#e8dcc4` with woven/paper texture note, no external URLs
- Stamp/badge: circular 1px dashed border, small-caps label, rotated ≤2deg
- Focus: 2px solid `#a34722` outline offset 2px
