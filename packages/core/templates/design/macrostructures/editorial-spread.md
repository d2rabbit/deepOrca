# Macrostructure: Editorial Spread

> Page skeleton fingerprint — visual system agnostic.

## Skeleton fingerprint

- **Title placement**: spread composition — headline occupies one column
  (often the left), the visual or body entry occupies the facing column;
  page number / section slug in a corner as furniture.
- **Separation language**: the gutter between the two columns IS the
  separation; thin column rules and folio lines allowed, cards forbidden.
- **Button voice**: "continue reading →" and "next spread →" links only,
  set as text with underline on hover.
- **Image handling**: one dominant image per spread, bled to one edge; a
  small secondary image may counterweight the headline column.
- **Reveal**: page-turn semantics — spreads cross-fade or slide as whole
  units; nothing animates independently inside a spread.

## Layout math

- Two unequal columns (e.g. 5/7 or 4/8) per spread; consecutive spreads
  mirror the ratio (7/5) to create a turning rhythm.
- Vertical grid shared across spreads: baseline positions repeat so flipping
  feels like the same magazine.

## Use when

Lookbooks, annual reports, case studies, photo essays, product stories told
in chapters. When the content is inherently pairwise (text + visual per
unit), a spread beats a scrolling column.

## Do not

- more than 2 columns of body text;
- centering everything — spreads are asymmetric by definition;
- mixing spread units with a bento or card zone in the same page.
