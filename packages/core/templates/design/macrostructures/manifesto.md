# Macrostructure: Manifesto

> Page skeleton fingerprint — visual system agnostic.

## Skeleton fingerprint

- **Title placement**: there may be no title at all — the first sentence IS
  the page. If a title exists it is one word or one number, set enormous.
- **Separation language**: each statement is its own full-viewport-ish block;
  separation is scale contrast between blocks, not lines or boxes.
- **Button voice**: at most ONE action in the entire page, at the very end,
  stated as a sentence, not a component ("Come build it with us →").
- **Image handling**: none, or a single full-bleed image/video block used as
  punctuation between statements.
- **Reveal**: statements appear in strict reading order; optional one-time
  entrance per block, never looping or parallax.

## Layout math

- 3-6 statements total; alternate alignment (left / center / right) or scale
  (huge / medium) between consecutive statements to create rhythm.
- Generous vertical gaps (≥25vh) — the page is mostly whitespace with
  sentences suspended in it.

## Use when

Mission pages, product launches with a point of view, open letters, causes,
"I am leaving / we are shutting down" posts. The structure argues; it does
not inform.

## Do not

- Feature lists, stats rows, or navigation mid-page;
- more than two font sizes per statement;
- hedging language — a manifesto commits.
